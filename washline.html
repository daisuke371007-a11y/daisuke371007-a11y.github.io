HOW TO PAINT

Drag a brush from its paint well across the paper. Every brush must end in a frame - and arrive in the colour that frame asks for.

Pass over a pigment drop and the brush picks it up: red and yellow make orange, yellow and blue make green, blue and red make purple, all three make umber.

Paint every square of paper exactly once. Later you'll meet stencils (the brush must be that colour as it passes), soap (washes the brush clear) and currents (cross only one way).

Every puzzle has exactly one solution. Drag back to undo a stroke; touch a well to start that brush again.

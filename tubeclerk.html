THE CLERK'S HANDBOOK

The spike. Every desk keeps its figures on a spike: a stack of paper slips. You can only ever touch the top. It holds 10 slips. Each slip holds a whole number from -9999 to 9999.

The clock. Every desk does one instruction per tick, all at the same time. A program that runs off its last line starts again from the top.

Trays. Slips arrive in trays A and B and must be filed, in order, in trays X and Y. A wrong slip stops the run at once.

Tubes. Four pneumatic tubes connect every desk. A tube holds one slip. A slip sent on one tick can be taken out on the next. SEND waits while a tube is full; RECV waits while it is empty.

The ledger. Sixteen shared cells, 0 to 15, readable and writable by every desk.

Filing. FILE IT runs your desks against the visible test and three hidden ones. Your score is the average number of ticks, how many instructions you used, and how many desks. Beat the bureau's PAR to earn its medals.

Writing. One instruction per line. name: marks a label. Anything after # or ; is a comment.

Keys: F10 step · Shift+F10 back · F5 run · F6 fast · F9 file it · Esc reset

KNOCKWORKS

Every other pachinko game hands you a board.
This one makes you build it.
Grade 0

HOW TO PLAY

You own a works. Every round the office sets a quota, and the only way to make it is the box in front of you.

Tap a part belowPick it up, then tap a square to bolt it in Tap a bolted partTake it back out. Nothing is permanent R / right-clickTurn the part you are holding DROPStart the round. Then tap along the top to release each ball SpaceDrop, and drop the next ball

Chain is the whole game. Every part a ball touches raises its chain, and every point it scores is multiplied by that chain. A ball that bounces twenty times is worth far more than four balls that fall straight through.

Multipliers are best placed high: everything the ball earns after it passes one is multiplied.

Parts wear as they are hit, so a ball trapped in one pocket stops paying. Spread the work around the box.

Miss a quota and you get one warning. Miss a second time and the works shuts down.

HOW TO PLAY

Aim by dragging (or moving the mouse) above your launcher, then release / click to fire. Keyboard: A/D or arrows to aim, Space to fire.

Every orb deals damage to the glass it hits. Blocks move down one row after every shot. Any block that reaches the bottom hits you for its remaining HP.

Your launcher moves to where your first orb lands. Grab +1 Orb rings for extra orbs on this floor, and coins for gold.

Bomb blocks explode into their neighbors. Armored blocks need their plates knocked off first. Clear a floor to pick an upgrade. Shops appear after floors 3 and 6. Beat the boss on floor 9 to win the act.

During a shot: FAST (F) speeds it up, RECALL (R) ends it early. Esc pauses. The run saves between shots.

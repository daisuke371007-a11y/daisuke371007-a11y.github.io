HOW TO WEAVE

Every order is a piece of cloth. Your job is to work out the draft that weaves it - the plan a weaver sets up on the loom.

Threading (top): every warp end - every column - is threaded through one shaft.
Tie-up (top right): every treadle lifts some shafts.
Treadling (right): every pick - every row - presses one treadle.

Where a lifted shaft holds an end, the warp shows on top. Everywhere else the weft shows. The cloth in the middle updates as you go; press Weave when it matches the order.

Colours: paint warp colours along the top and weft colours down the right with yarn from the basket.

One star for a match, one for using no more shafts than needed, one for no more treadles. Click and drag to draw lines. Repeat copies what you've drawn so far across the rest; As drawn in copies the threading into the treadling.

HOW TO PLAY

Enemies spiral in from the rim toward your core. Anything that touches the core damages it.

Build: pick a turret from the list (or the bar at the bottom), then tap an empty slot on a ring. Turrets ride their ring as it turns. Tap a turret you own to upgrade or sell it.

Conjunction: when two turrets on different rings line up on the same spoke, they fire one huge beam along that line. This is where most of your damage comes from.

Spin a ring: drag a ring (or use 1 / 2 / 3 then A / D) to turn it yourself and force those line-ups. Spinning costs spin energy, which refills on its own.

Core Pulse (button or Q) blasts everything close to the core and shoves it back. Space starts the wave, F fast-forwards, Esc pauses. Your run saves between waves.

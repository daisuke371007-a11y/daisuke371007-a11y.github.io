SNAPLINE

Everything on your line is bait. Sweep, lure, haul — don't let it snap.

HOW TO PLAY

Your line hangs from the boat. Anything the line touches on the way down gets hooked — and a line that just hangs there barely catches, so keep steering and sweep the line through the fish.

Everything you catch is bait. Every fish on your line lures more fish in from further away — even the junk, especially the junk. An old boot spinning in the dark pulls harder than a sardine. Stingers follow the bait too.

Deeper fish are worth far more and weigh far more. Land a whole haul at once for a big multiplier. If the tension bar fills, the line snaps and that catch is gone.

Deep water pays far more per fish. To get down there fast, hold SLACK: an empty slack line dives, and it hooks nothing on the way, so you can drop straight past the sardines.

After about 55 seconds the line starts to fray on its own, and slack will not save it. Don't dawdle down there.

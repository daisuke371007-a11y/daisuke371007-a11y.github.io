HOW TO PLAY

The horde marches down toward your keep. Tap a standing domino to knock it over. It falls the way it points, knocks the next one, and every domino in the chain crushes whatever it lands on — plus a shockwave to both sides.

Longer chains hit harder. Every domino later in a chain carries more momentum, and long chains pay out gold.

Fallen dominoes stand back up on their own after a few seconds, so timing is everything: wait until the horde is walking along your line, then tap.

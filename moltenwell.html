HOW TO PLAY

Monsters climb the well toward your hearth at the top. You cannot build walls or towers — you pour materials, and they behave like real sand, water and lava.

Hold / dragPour the selected material wherever you point 1 – 8Pick a material SpaceSend in the next wave EscPause

Pressure (the blue bar) is your only limit. It refills on its own, so pour in bursts.

Everything drains into the abyss at the bottom, but it piles up on the rock shelves on the way down. A pool of lava on a shelf keeps burning things for a long time.

Materials react. Lava and water make stone, which falls and crushes. Lava and sand make glass. Fire runs along oil. Acid eats stone, armour and sand. Ice freezes a whole pool. Find them all in the Reactions list.

Monsters are not all the same: emberlings walk through fire, oozers shrug off water, carapaces ignore crushing but melt in acid, and mothlings fly over everything — but steam scalds them.

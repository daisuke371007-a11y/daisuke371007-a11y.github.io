BEAMWRIGHT

Push the mirrors. Bring the light to every lamp.
Never stand where the light will be.

HOW TO PLAY

Each box holds a lamp housing that throws a beam of light, and one or more glass lamps. Light every lamp at once and the box is done.

You light them by pushing things: mirrors turn the beam, iron shutters stop it, and half-silvered glass splits it in two. You can push one thing at a time, and nothing can be pulled.

The light burns. You can never step into the beam, and you can never make a move that would send the beam through where you stand. Building the light's path closes your own.

Every box has a par: the fewest moves it can possibly be solved in, found by checking every position the box can reach. Match it for a star. Nobody can beat it.
